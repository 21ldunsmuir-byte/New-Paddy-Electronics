The way we work

No courier, no call centre. Just a counter.

We’ve been at this since 2008. You talk to the technician who fixes your device, and it never leaves our hands.

№ 1

Genuine parts only

Every screen, battery and port we fit meets manufacturer standard. No grey-market panels, no surprises six weeks later.

№ 2

Most repairs same day

Screens and batteries are usually done while you wait. Anything longer, we tell you exactly when to come back.

№ 3

The quote is the price

Free diagnosis, a written quote before we open anything, and not a cent more when you collect. No hidden fees, ever.

№ 4

Certified technicians

Sixteen years and ten thousand repairs deep. Micro-soldering, board-level diagnostics and liquid damage are everyday work here.
